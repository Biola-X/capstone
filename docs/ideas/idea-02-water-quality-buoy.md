-   :material-lightbulb-on: **Capstone Project Idea**

    ---

    A solar-powered floating buoy that continuously logs water temperature, pH,
    turbidity, and dissolved oxygen for a local watershed or coastal partner, and
    uploads readings over cellular to a public dashboard. Target build cost well
    under commercial equivalents. Challenges: waterproofing and biofouling,
    power budgeting for year-round operation, sensor calibration and drift, and
    mooring design.
