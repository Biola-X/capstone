## Form questions (for reference)

If the embedded form does not load in your browser, these are the fields it asks
for. Use the button above to open it directly.

**Form:** *Biola University: 2026-27 Engineering Senior Capstone Project Proposal*

| # | Question | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Name** | Short answer | Yes |
| 2 | **Email** | Short answer | Yes |
| 3 | **Project Proposal or Idea** — a brief summary and the needs it addresses | Paragraph | Yes |
| 4 | **Client Involvement** — willing to serve as project client, meeting with the team 1–2× per month? | Multiple choice: *Yes* / *No* / *Maybe, I need more information* | Yes |
| 5 | **Financial Sponsorship** — is your company willing to sponsor, and what is the approximate budget? | Paragraph | Yes |
| 6 | **Prototype Ownership** — will you keep the completed student work / prototype? | Paragraph | Yes |
| 7 | **Additional Thoughts or Questions** | Paragraph | No |

!!! tip "Have several ideas?"
    You do not need to submit the form once per idea. Add each idea to the
    [Project Ideas](ideas/index.md) section of this site and reference this site
    in your form submission.
