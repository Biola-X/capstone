-   :material-lightbulb-on: **Capstone Project Idea**

    ---

    A configurable, low-cost input device that lets gamers with limited hand
    mobility remap buttons and joystick axes to large external switches, foot
    pedals, or sip-and-puff inputs. Presents to a PC or console as a standard
    controller. Challenges: ergonomics for a specific user, latency, a friendly
    remapping app, and a 3D-printable modular enclosure.
