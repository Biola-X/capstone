    Summer Engineering Internships (SEI)¶

    Part of the Biola-X initiative.

    The Summer Engineering Internship program places one or two students on a focused engineering problem for 8–10 weeks over the summer, working with a faculty advisor and — where there is one — an industry or community partner. Each internship ends with a working deliverable and a short technical report.

    This page is an archive of completed SEI projects.
